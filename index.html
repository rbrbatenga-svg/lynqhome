02 / Documentation

Setup & Troubleshooting Guide

Tap your physical LYNQ card against the back of an iPhone or Android phone. When the link loads, click the Sign In button at the bottom to connect your Google account and link the card to your profile permanently.
Social media apps like Messenger or Facebook open links inside restrictive internal browser windows that block authentication popups. This can temporarily hide your editing privileges.
Tap the menu dots in the top right corner of your app and select "Open in Chrome" or "Open in Safari". Alternatively, copy your card link and paste it into a private or Incognito browser tab.
